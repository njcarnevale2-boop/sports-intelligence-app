# Sports Intelligence OS

The Sports Intelligence Operating System is a decision-support platform for serious NFL bettors and fantasy football players.

Our mission is simple:

Transform hours of sports research into minutes of confident decision-making.

## Product Philosophy

We are not building a picks service.

We are building a sports decision platform.

Every recommendation should answer:

- What changed?
- Why does it matter?
- What should the user do?
